    Lab Design Project

    Designed the layout and function of the QA and engineering labs, including the configuration of network switches and routers to optimize video over IP testing. The equipment was racked in a modular fashion to support small test beds of four video services and scale up to 128 video services. Test bed reconfiguration times decreased by more than 50%, significantly cutting down on labor and time costs associated with setup. It increased the efficiency of lab usage, simplified network management, and allowed for future bandwidth upgrades.

    Network Manager Testing Project

    Executed a detailed analysis of the network manager's performance at scale. The goal was to assess its performance and robustness as the system scaled up. Using device simulators to build test systems, a test matrix was created to cover every potential system configuration. Redundant tests and configurations with insignificant results were identified and subsequently eliminated. This process reduced the test matrix by 60%, saving time and providing more accurate information on the system’s scaling and processing limits for sales and publications.
